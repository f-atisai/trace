# TRACE Code of Conduct

TRACE adopts the **Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 3.0**, as the standard for participation in this project.

The complete Contributor Covenant 3.0 is permanently available at:

https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/

By participating in TRACE community spaces, contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and other participants agree to follow that Code of Conduct.

## Project expectations

In keeping with the Contributor Covenant, TRACE aims to provide a welcoming, safe, equitable, and professionally collaborative community. Participants should engage kindly and honestly, respect different viewpoints and experiences, give and receive constructive feedback, take responsibility for their contributions, and help repair harm when it occurs.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, inappropriate sexualization, disclosure of private information, threats or encouragement of harm, deliberate impersonation, failure to credit contributed sources, and other conduct that threatens the well-being of the community are not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in TRACE project spaces and when an individual is officially representing TRACE in public or other spaces.

## Reporting

Possible Code of Conduct violations should be reported privately to the TRACE project maintainer using the contact method published on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public GitHub issue to report sensitive conduct concerns.

Reports will be handled as promptly, fairly, and confidentially as reasonably possible. The privacy and safety of people reporting incidents will be prioritized.

## Enforcement

TRACE follows the Contributor Covenant 3.0 approach to addressing and repairing harm. Depending on the circumstances and severity of a violation, project maintainers may use measures ranging from a private warning or temporary limitation of participation to temporary suspension or permanent removal from project community spaces.

Enforcement decisions should consider the impact on the people involved and the community as a whole, with safety, fairness, proportionality, and opportunities to repair harm where appropriate.
